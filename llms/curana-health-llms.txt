# Curana Health

> Curana Health is a value-based senior-living healthcare company delivering on-site primary care, behavioral health, palliative care, and chronic-condition management to 250,000+ residents across 2,000+ communities in 30+ states. For CMS-9115-F / ONC 21st Century Cures Act compliance it publishes a standards-based FHIR R4 Interoperability (Patient Access) API that lets Medicare Advantage members authorize third-party apps to read their claims, encounter, and clinical data over OAuth 2.0, plus a public provider-directory surface.

## APIs

- [Curana Health Interoperability API](https://curanahealth.com/interoperability-api/): FHIR R4 (4.0.1) Patient Access API. Base URL https://fhir.curanahealth.com/v1/api. Read-only GET, offset/limit pagination, FHIR Bundle responses, OAuth 2.0 member authorization.
